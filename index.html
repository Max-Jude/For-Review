Our Legacy

Brief History of De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy

Established with a foundational commitment to educational distinction and moral rectitude, De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy opened its doors as a sanctuary dedicated to the total development of the young mind. From its modest beginnings, the institution was built on the firm belief that true education harmonizes intellectual mastery with character building.

Over the decades, De Wisdom has expanded into a full-fledged comprehensive citadel of learning, featuring state-of-the-art science laboratories, modern ICT centers, extensive sports facilities, and an enriched curriculum. Guided by seasoned master educators, the academy continues to raise generations of scholars who consistently achieve outstanding academic milestones, demonstrate exemplary civic leadership, and excel in higher institutions worldwide.

🎯
